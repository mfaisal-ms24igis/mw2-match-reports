Tournament report

Five maps. Five losses. One captain with a K/D of 0.38.

Dr.Evil drew first blood in all five games. That was as good as it got for the Allies. Nuketown was the closest they came: 16–12 up at four minutes, then outscored 13–4 in the next one. Across about 50 minutes of play, the Allies were ahead for a total of 5 minutes 10 seconds.

STRIKER finished with 133 kills. Capt. Mandalorian won the captains’ head-to-head 26–9. Capt. TrOjaN went 30 kills and 79 deaths, died to STRIKER 40 times, left game three early, and scored twice in the final.
